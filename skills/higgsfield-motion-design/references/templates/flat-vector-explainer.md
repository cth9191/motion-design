## Source and deliberate differences

Nine source phases follow one person through three different service contexts, with a fixed camera and prop morphs. The three environments must become actual use cases for the new subject. The original price and trial offer are not template defaults.

Resolve these semantic fields from the campaign and source material. Numbered/ranged fields mean one concrete binding for each use. Replace every `{{...}}` before submission. Include a per-shot exact-copy list, relevant input assets and expected end state. Never submit this file with unresolved variables. Follow [template-adaptation.md](../template-adaptation.md) for the mapping and fidelity check.

## Asset plan

Apply these asset suggestions only when identity accuracy or the selected model requires them. Prompt-only is valid when sufficient; prefer existing assets and add generated references only for a specific need. Distinct shot compositions do not require an image or video job per shot.

Resolve the character sheet, three actual contexts and compatible prop morphs before rendering. A style/character reference may help, but this preset needs no photoreal hero. Composite exact type and factual app steps when generation cannot keep them stable.

## Default design bindings

Brand violet #6C2BFF, first context coral #FF5A4E, second yellow #FFC838, third aqua #21D0C3, ink #1B1B3A, off-white #FFF8F0, highlight #C6F432 and flat skin fill #E8A87C. Reference type staggers about 30 ms, marker swipes last 200 ms and option pins enter at approximately 60 ms spacing. Reconcile these to delivery frames.

## Audio branch

The source prompt is silent. For a new launch/motion campaign, replace its silence clauses with instrumental music and synchronized effects and record that departure. Preserve an explicit silent request. Do not add narration by default.

## Parameterized mapping aid — adapt the original text above

```text
{{FORMAT}} | {{BRAND_OR_SUBJECT}} | {{AUDIO_SPEC}}

WORLD, SUBJECT AND DESIGN
Use pure flat vector construction: large circles, rounded rectangles, chunky limbs, simple eyes and solid fills. {{CHARACTER_REFERENCE}} recurs unchanged. Represent {{APP_OBJECT}} as a simplified, truthful graphic workflow rather than a fictitious screenshot. Assign {{CONTEXT_A}}, {{CONTEXT_B}} and {{CONTEXT_C}} distinct saturated backgrounds, plus a light neutral home world and a strong brand-color finish. No dimensional lighting or perspective-camera effects. A flat ground-contact ellipse is sufficient. Use bold geometric type and a single accent swipe behind the selected word or phrase.

CAMERA, EDITING AND MOTION
Keep the camera locked. Energy comes from elastic poses, hand-drawn smear shapes, object scale, prop morphs and hard background switches. Use up to twenty percent squash/stretch, anticipation before leaps and overshoot that settles. Lead each transition with one known prop so the audience follows the transformation. Do not add optical blur: fast actions use drawn smears. Background changes support the three use cases; do not replace them with unrelated decorative flashing.

TIMELINE — default 15-second adaptation; retime explicitly for other delivery lengths.

[0.00–1.60s] Phase 1 — constrained character
On a light background, show the character with a defeated posture. Drop three {{PROBLEM_PROPS}} around them in sequence, each landing with compression and a small reactive hop. Place the short problem headline above with the selected accent phrase.

[1.60–3.20s] Phase 2 — discover the options
Slide {{APP_OBJECT}} into the character’s hand at deliberately oversized scale. Populate five simple options or pins in a stagger. Enlarge the eyes briefly in response, then settle. Replace the headline with the new opportunity. Show actual supported choices, not invented integrations.

[3.20–4.80s] Phase 3 — first use case
Hard-switch to {{CONTEXT_A}}. Bring in {{PROP_A}} and perform {{ACTION_A}} twice or in two distinct beats. The prop visibly responds to the character. Land the first {{STATUS_CHIPS}} entry clear of the action.

[4.80–6.40s] Phase 4 — first prop morph
Stretch {{PROP_A}} into {{PROP_B}} with recognizable intermediate geometry while switching to {{CONTEXT_B}}. Have the same character perform {{ACTION_B}}, briefly overshoot, then balance. Show the second status chip and a headline expressing continuity across contexts.

[6.40–8.00s] Phase 5 — second prop morph
Roll, fold or reshape {{PROP_B}} into {{PROP_C}} as the world switches to {{CONTEXT_C}}. Perform {{ACTION_C}} in a clear side-on silhouette. Use simple geometric particles only when they explain the action. Show the third status chip.

[8.00–9.60s] Phase 6 — one system connects the cases
Return the character to the light world with a confident landing. Orbit three rounded cards representing the three contexts around them. Show the same three contexts together inside {{APP_OBJECT}}, with completion marks where truthful. Land the unifying headline.

[9.60–11.20s] Phase 7 — three-step workflow
Scale the app object itself to about seventy percent of the frame; camera stays fixed. Show {{WORKFLOW_STEPS}} as three large, distinct actions: choose, inspect, confirm or the actual equivalent. A cursor/hand presses the final button with compression and rebound, producing simple confetti. Include a price only when verified.

[11.20–12.80s] Phase 8 — repeatable freedom
Clear the confetti and send the recurring character across frame in a joyful run. Cycle the three context colors and their relevant props behind successive strides. Keep the final benefit headline screen-locked and readable while the background changes.

[12.80–15.00s] Phase 9 — branded resolution
Switch to the brand background. The character skids and settles beside the app object, which transforms into {{BRAND}}. Add {{BRAND_LINE}} and {{CTA}} by 13.5 seconds, then hold through 14.3. Pop the graphic pieces away in a short stagger through 14.6, fade the clean background through 14.9, and leave a 0.1-second empty tail. No inherited free-trial offer.

COPY AND REFERENCES
{{EXACT_COPY_BY_SHOT}}. No other readable text. Use {{ASSET_BINDINGS}} with their declared identity/style/start/end/overlay roles. Preserve real product identity, supported claims and specified component geometry.

DELIVERY
{{DELIVERY_AND_FINISHING_PLAN}}. Audio follows {{AUDIO_SPEC}} throughout. Submit all timed shots and shared rules as one complete-film request. Separate clips are an accepted fallback only, as described in production.md.
```

## Fidelity checks

- All three contexts depict real uses of the subject.
- Morphs connect the same prop family and the same character survives every switch.
- The phone close-up is an element scale, not a moving camera.
- Pricing, availability and CTA have no inherited demo claims.

Compare these checks with the rendered motion, not just the prompt. A missing signature action is a failed shot even if its materials look polished. Repair that shot or report the specific departure; do not relabel a generic result as this preset.
