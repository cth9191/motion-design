# Editorial collage

Preset ID: `editorial-collage`. Aliases: paper collage, editorial, science explainer, recorded voice.

Paper cutouts, bold type, and graphic diagrams explain one surprising cause and effect.

Best for: Educational shorts, thought leadership, and concise concept explainers.
