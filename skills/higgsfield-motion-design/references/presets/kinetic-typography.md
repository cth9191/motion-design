# Kinetic typography

Preset ID: `kinetic-typography`. Aliases: kinetic type, animated text, type animation, typography.

A fixed-frame sequence of headline builds, six image cards, an icon bridge, and a scrolling capability list.

Best for: Announcements, offers, courses, and software launches.

**Original starting prompt:** [Read the exact source](../source-prompts/kinetic-typography.txt).

**Required next read:** [Full production template](../templates/kinetic-typography.md). The card description is for browsing only; do not generate from it or replace the full template with a short style summary.

## Intake

Essential: A message or something to promote.

Optional: Exact wording, Brand colors or logo, Photos for the floating cards.

Reuse the existing campaign and available website/assets. Do not ask the user to supply production terminology, timings or camera instructions.

## Source structure

The published prompt specifies a 10-second continuous flat composition with a final hold, despite being described as a loop. Default adaptation stretches its five phases to 15 seconds. It is not a seamless loop unless a reset is explicitly designed; do not claim that a final headline matches the sparse opening.

## Adaptation

Follow [template-adaptation.md](../template-adaptation.md). Preserve the selected template’s shot/cut structure, camera behavior, material contrast, transition mechanics and ending. Replace its subject, story, actual assets, exact copy and factual claims. User instructions override template defaults; record deliberate departures.
