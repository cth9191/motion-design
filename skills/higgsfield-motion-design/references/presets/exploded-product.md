# Exploded product

Preset ID: `exploded-product`. Aliases: watch, exploded view, component fly-through, macro fly-through.

Parts separate along one axis while macro camera moves reveal the product inside.

Best for: Watches, audio hardware, electronics, and engineered products.

**Original starting prompt:** [Read the exact source](../source-prompts/exploded-product.txt).

Essential: A product reference and the components or features to show.

Optional: Verified specifications, CAD views or teardown images, Brand artwork.

Reuse the existing campaign and available website/assets. Do not ask the user to supply production terminology, timings or camera instructions.
