App launch film, 16:9, 30fps, 15 seconds, SUPER 2D VECTOR MOTION — flat illustration style: bold geometric shapes, solid fills, no gradients except where locked, no outlines, no textures, no 3D, no photorealism. Everything is crisp vector: rounded rectangles, perfect circles, chunky limbs, oversized simple shapes. Motion is snappy shape-morph animation: elements stretch, squash, morph into one another, and backgrounds swap color in hard full-frame switches ON the beat. Fully silent piece — no soundtrack, no sound design; all rhythm is carried by cuts, morphs and color switches alone.

Nothing eases linearly — everything overshoots and settles. One clear leading element per transition.
