University launch film, 16:9, 30fps, 15 seconds, HYBRID STYLE: premium 3D CGI render fused with flat 2D vector motion. The 3D layer — physical objects of the university (buildings, books, cap, medal) — rendered Octane-grade: PBR materials, soft studio lighting, real physics, slow-mo moments. The 2D layer — characters, doodles, UI chips, scribble arrows — is pure flat vector with bold shapes and no outlines, animated with snappy squash-stretch, living ON TOP of and AROUND the 3D objects: vector characters walk on rendered surfaces, flat doodles orbit real geometry. The two layers stay visually distinct — 3D is glossy and lit, 2D is flat and graphic — but physically interact: this contact IS the style. Fully silent piece — no soundtrack, no sound design; all rhythm is carried by motion timing and cuts alone.

STYLE CONSISTENCY (the core rule): 3D objects are ALWAYS fully lit, PBR, casting soft shadows, with real physics. 2D elements are ALWAYS flat solid fills, zero gradients, zero outlines, zero received lighting, zero cast shadows — even standing on glossy 3D surfaces (only mirror reflections of them may appear on 3D glass/chrome). The layers interact physically (books tilt under character hops, ribbon bounces on the flat chest) but never exchange render properties. Breaking this rule breaks the style.

CAMERA & MOTION: 3D beats use cinematic moves — the rise, 30-degree orbit, slow-mo cap toss, medal descent — always ease-out, always settling. 2D beats and elements use snappy cartoon timing: anticipation, smear frames, overshoot, hold. Hard cuts and match cuts on color or action only. No handheld, no shake.

RENDER: 3D — Octane-grade: matte concrete with fine micro-texture, acid glass spine with soft inner glow and real refraction, chrome medal with crisp reflections, cloth-sim tassel, soft contact shadows on clean floors. 2D — razor-sharp flat vector at any scale, solid palette fills only. Acid #D1EF17 must match EXACTLY across both layers — the color is the bridge between worlds. No grain, no vignette, no lens flare — vibrant, premium, playful.
