## Asset plan

Apply these asset suggestions only when identity accuracy or the selected model requires them. Prompt-only is valid when sufficient; prefer existing assets and add generated references only for a specific need. Distinct shot compositions do not require an image or video job per shot.

Collect the factual explanation and data sources first. Prepare a consistent set of subject cutouts and diagram pieces. A common baseline and denominator are mandatory for numeric comparisons; never reuse the source’s 50/50 illustration as scientific evidence. If narration is requested, write one short source-backed script and align its ideas to the four compositions. For silent delivery, revise headlines/diagrams so the explanation still works without speech.

## Default design bindings

Main backgrounds #2252FF, #FF6728 and #FFE14A, with black #000000 and a bound warm cream. Use one dominant background per composition, matte paper and stable upper-left shadows. Blue/orange can identify two paths only when the factual explanation supports that distinction.

## Audio branch

By default, use instrumental rhythm and paper/diagram accents without speech. If narration is requested, write a concise explanation in four semantic blocks: surprise/question, two pathways, isolated mechanism, changed outcome. Start promptly and complete the conclusion; align the paper actions to meaning. Supporting plucked bass, dry percussion and sparse mallet notes can provide rhythm below the voice. Use short paper/trigger accents. Do not inherit the source’s scientific script or numerical assumptions without verification. Silent delivery must explain the same story through its diagrams and copy.

## Fidelity checks

- Four distinct compositions and three meaningful shape-match cuts remain.
- Path geometry explains the actual topic, with one shared subject and correct causal order.
- Any numeric label agrees with the diagram from its first visible frame.
- Silent delivery remains understandable without the source narration.
- Ending resolves the opening question and retains only a small relevant motion.

Compare these checks with the rendered motion, not just the prompt. A missing signature action is a failed shot even if its materials look polished. Repair that shot or report the specific departure; do not relabel a generic result as this preset.
