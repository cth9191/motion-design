# Footage + graphics

Preset ID: `footage-overlays`. Aliases: motion on footage, presenter, overlays, talking head, real footage.

Purposeful graphic overlays support a presenter or real-world scene without replacing it.

Best for: Talking-head content, product demos, tutorials, and founder updates.

**Original starting prompt:** [Read the exact source](../source-prompts/footage-overlays.txt).

**Required next read:** [Full production template](../templates/footage-overlays.md). The card description is for browsing only; do not generate from it or replace the full template with a short style summary.

## Intake

Essential: The source video and the message the graphics should support.

Optional: Exact overlay copy, Brand colors, Whether to retain the source speech.

Reuse the existing campaign and available website/assets. Do not ask the user to supply production terminology, timings or camera instructions.

## Source structure

The source prompt uses two camera setups: a static first view and a newly generated second angle at 5 seconds. It also requests cloned speech. Selecting this visual preset does not request identity cloning, rewritten speech or a synthetic angle. Preserve the two-view structure when suitable footage exists; otherwise keep the supplied footage and record the visual departure. Neither of the two published preview URLs is verified as the input attachment.

## Adaptation

Follow [template-adaptation.md](../template-adaptation.md). Preserve the selected template’s shot/cut structure, camera behavior, material contrast, transition mechanics and ending. Replace its subject, story, actual assets, exact copy and factual claims. User instructions override template defaults; record deliberate departures.

Default delivery: 15 seconds, 16:9, 30fps, instrumental music and synchronized effects, no narration. Native source and model settings can differ. The full template contains the actual ending and timing.
