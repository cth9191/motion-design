SCENE CONTEXT
A 15-second fully silent architectural launch film for the fictional residential development "HIGGS PARK", executed as ONE single unbroken camera move. The film is a living blueprint that progressively extrudes into photoreal archviz. There are no cuts, no dissolves, no fades, no editorial wipes anywhere: every scene change is caused by a visible in-world event — the drawing's own linework turning to matter, an extrusion, a plotted overlay — happening inside the scene while the camera keeps moving. Drawing and reality are the same geometry: every plotted line lands exactly where its material twin appears.

WORLD / HERO
"HIGGS PARK" — invented architecture, referencing no real building, developer or architect: a 12-storey stepped-terrace tower, each floor set back, creating cascading planted terraces; a continuous chartreuse acid #D1EF17 balcony ribbon rail as the signature line in both worlds; warm concrete body #CFC8BC; full-height glazing with true sky reflections; oak #B08954 double-height timber lobby; greenery #5E8C5A. Accent color definition, locked for the entire film: chartreuse acid #D1EF17 is a COLD yellow-green, sitting between highlighter yellow and fresh lime, always green-biased, in every beat and under every lighting condition. Blueprint world: deep blueprint paper #16233F, faint millimeter grid rgba(234,242,255,0.12), draft-white #EAF2FF linework and type, chartreuse acid #D1EF17 dimension lines and accent words. The drawing layer is honest 2D vector CAD language: crisp 1px lines, dimension arrows with ticks, section hatching, level marks, title-block stamps, zero glow.

FIRST FRAME
Blueprint-deep paper fills the entire frame, the faint millimeter grid breathing at low opacity. Nothing else is in frame. Frame center holds the exact spot where the chartreuse centerline is about to plot.

FORMAT
16:9 horizontal, 30fps, 15.0 seconds, one continuous take.

OPTICS
84° diagonal field of view, classic wide coverage. Lens locked for the entire 15 seconds — one lens, one take.
STRONG anamorphic lens character: horizontal squeeze and compression, oval elliptical bokeh, horizontally stretched highlights, curved barrel edge distortion, chromatic aberration toward the edges. NO lens flares, NO light streaks, NO floating bokeh circles.
All type, dimensions and title-block graphics stay composed inside the central 70% of frame so barrel distortion and edge aberration never touch readable text.

CAMERA — ONE UNBROKEN MOVE
The camera begins static and face-on to the drawing plane, like a rostrum camera over a drafting table. As the section drawing tilts into 3D, the camera arcs right and enters space with it. Through the ink-to-matter rise it performs a slow rising clockwise orbit around the tower, climbing in sync with the floor-by-floor ignition. The orbit crests above a mid-height terrace and tilts to plumb vertical, looking straight down; it then descends vertically through the extruding floor plan into the apartment, settling into a gentle eye-height forward glide toward the glazing. The glide passes straight through the glass onto the terrace, then reverses into one long continuous pull-back and rise that never stops until the end — carrying the proof overlay, the golden-hour wide and the final lockup where the tower recedes to frame center. All motion eases out; every move blends into the next with curved velocity — the camera never fully stops until the final 1.0-second hold. No handheld, no shake, no speed ramps.

ACTION TIMING — transitions are in-world events, never cuts
0.0–1.8s — On the blueprint paper a single chartreuse centerline plots itself left-to-right through frame center; from it the building SECTION draws at plot speed — floor slabs, stepped terraces, stair runs — draft-white lines with hatching filling the wall cuts, chartreuse dimension arrows snapping between level marks with ticks. A title stamp rotates into the bottom-right title block: "HIGGS PARK — REV.01". The headline plots above the section stroke by stroke, 400ms per line: "Drawn to be lived in." — the word "lived" plotted in chartreuse.
SEAM A (no cut): the finished section drawing itself tilts — the drawing plane rotates into 3D space with every line continuously visible throughout the rotation.
1.8–3.6s — The section resolves into a full axonometric of the stepped tower — still pure draft-white wireframe on blueprint paper — self-building floor by floor, 12 storeys stacking upward at plot speed. Two labels leader-line to features: "TERRACE EVERY FLOOR" · "3.1 M CEILINGS". The camera is already arcing in the slow orbit it will hold.
SEAM B (no cut): materialization begins inside the linework itself at street level — the drawing's own strokes start turning to matter; there is never a screen-space boundary.
3.6–5.8s — THE INK-TO-MATTER RISE: the building materializes THROUGH its own drawing, like current running through a circuit. On the ground floor the chartreuse ribbon line ignites first — the plotted stroke charges and thickens into painted metal along its own exact path, acting as the fuse for the whole floor: from the ribbon, matter flows outward through the connected linework — slab-edge lines swell into concrete with visible mass, mullion lines crystallize glass between them with sky reflections arriving as it sets, terrace outlines sprout real planting. Each floor ignites this way in sequence, bottom to top, in the same rhythm as the wireframe self-build — always the lines themselves turning to matter, floor by floor, element by element. Above the highest ignited floor the tower is still pure wireframe on blueprint paper. Around the finished floors the blueprint paper develops like a cyanotype exposed to real sunlight: the deep blue photochemically brightens into true sky, blooming outward from the building's silhouette in a soft photographic development and completing by the end of the beat. The camera's rising orbit climbs with the ignition so the transformation always lives near frame center. Headline: "From line to life." — "life." in chartreuse.
SEAM C (no cut): the rising orbit crests above a mid-height terrace and the camera tilts to plumb vertical — and in the straight-down view perspective flattens into a top-down apartment PLAN: the floor below reads as blueprint linework again, furniture drawn as line symbols on paper.
5.8–7.8s — The ignition reaches the plan vertically: as the camera descends through the ceiling plane, the plan's lines charge and extrude upward past the lens into a photoreal apartment — oak floor rolling out as if the material is being laid in real time, the sofa inflating from its line symbol, daylight entering through full-height glazing. One dimension line remains floating in reality — chartreuse, holding "42 m²" — a ghost of the drawing. The descent settles into a gentle eye-height forward glide toward the glazing.
SEAM D (no cut): the glide continues straight through the full-height glass onto the terrace — the pane passes cleanly across the lens, no distortion spike.
7.8–9.6s — Balcony beat: photoreal planted terrace, the chartreuse rail crisp in sunlight, the stepped terraces of neighbors cascading below. Three drafting labels plot on with leader lines, coexisting with reality: "PLANTED TERRACE — 11 M²" · "WEST SUN AFTER 15:00" · "PET-PROOF RAIL". The camera begins the long pull-back it will hold to the end.
9.6–11.4s — PROOF OVERLAY (no peel, no wipe): the pull-back continues uninterrupted; over the photoreal building, draft-white and chartreuse linework plots itself directly ON TOP of the built geometry — every line landing exactly on its material twin: slab edges, mullion grids, the chartreuse ribbon rails, terrace outlines — a live dimensional overlay, with two or three chartreuse dimension arrows snapping between floor levels. For a beat the drawing and the building coexist in one frame as one object; then the lines sink into the surfaces they trace, absorbed into concrete, glass and metal, while the light warms continuously toward golden hour in one natural shift. Headline: "Exactly as promised." — "Exactly." in chartreuse.
11.4–13.2s — The continuing pull-back opens into the wide hero: full tower at golden hour, terraces glowing, chartreuse ribbons catching the low sun while holding their cold yellow-green hue against the warm light, gentle wind in the terrace planting, soft golden-hour volumetrics — while the faint blueprint grid remains softly visible in the SKY only, holding both worlds in one frame. Title-block chips plot down a corner column: "Q4 2027" · "1–4 BEDROOMS" · "FIRST 50 UNITS — PRE-LAUNCH PRICE".
13.2–15.0s — The same pull-back recedes the tower to frame center; all remaining linework elements slide and converge into a single underline that plots beneath the wordmark as it draws on in draft white: "HIGGS PARK" — underline in chartreuse. Secondary line in title-block lettering: "Live inside the drawing." Stamp slams bottom-right: "SALES OPEN". The camera finally stops. Hold 1.0 second, then the entire frame un-plots — every line retracting in reverse plot order — back to empty blueprint deep. Silent tail on the last frames.

Materialization always travels through the plotted linework itself, igniting floor by floor, element by element, bottom-to-top in one readable rhythm — never patchy, never appearing everywhere at once, never a moving edge crossing the screen.
